# Security policy

Ons handles intimate personal data: who people are, who they are looking for, and how they relate. Please report anything that could expose it **privately**, never in a public issue or pull request.

## How to report
Use GitHub's private reporting: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what data it could expose. Do not include real applicant data in the report.

## What counts
- Access to another applicant's identity (Instagram handle, name) before mutual acceptance
- Answers marked private being shown to other applicants or sent to a third party
- Bypassing authentication, rate limits or audit logging
- Any way to recover encrypted identity data
- Vulnerable dependencies with a realistic exploit path

## What happens next
You will get an acknowledgement, then a fix or a decision. Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it.
